Post Construction Cleaning in NYC: your questions

How do you price post construction cleaning?

By the size of the space and how much dust and debris is left. Send us photos for a quote.

Do you remove large debris?

We remove leftover small debris and trash bags. Large construction debris and dumpsters stay with your contractor.

Can you clean between trades?

Yes. Many contractors book a rough clean mid job and a final clean before the walk-through.

Can you provide a COI?

Yes, we issue a certificate of insurance for your building before the job.

Do I need to provide supplies?

No. We bring all supplies.
