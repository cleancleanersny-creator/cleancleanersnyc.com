The agreement

Between you (the Creator) and Clean Cleaners NYC LLC.

  1. What you receive. A complimentary Basic Cleaning of your home, for apartments up to 2 bedrooms. Bigger homes or extra services are quoted at a special partner price before booking. Clean Cleaners NYC sends you its next available slots and you choose one.
  2. What you share. On the day of your cleaning, 3 Instagram Stories: 1 before, 1 with our team working and 1 after. Within 5 days after the cleaning, 1 Reel posted as an Instagram Collab with @cleancleanersnyc.
  3. Disclosure. Your content shows your personal code and clearly discloses the partnership with #ad or the Paid Partnership label, as required by the FTC.
  4. Repost rights. You agree that Clean Cleaners NYC may repost and share your Stories and Reel, with credit to you, on our social media and website. The Reel stays up on your account for at least 6 months.
  5. Rewards. You earn $25 for every new client who books with your code and completes their first cleaning. Repeat bookings, self-referrals and cancelled jobs are not included. Rewards and level perks unlock once your Reel is live, and are paid monthly.
  6. Levels. 5 cleanings booked with your code unlock the Kitchen Experience (Steam Kitchen Deep Cleaning). 7 cleanings unlock Insider (Steam Bathroom Cleaning and Sanitizing). 10 cleanings unlock Ambassador (Full Deep Cleaning with steam).
  7. Scheduling. Please give us at least 24 hours notice to reschedule. A late cancellation or no-show uses up the complimentary cleaning. An adult must be home or give access, and pets should be secured.
  8. If content is not posted. If the 3 Stories and the Reel are not posted on time, your code is paused, you leave the Creator Club, and Clean Cleaners NYC may bill the standard price of the complimentary service.
  9. Honest content. Share only your real experience. No fake reviews, no promises of prices or services we do not offer, and no paid ads using our brand name without written approval.
  10. Our promise to you. A fully insured, BBB accredited team, the same care we give every client, and your address and personal details are never shared. You can leave the Club at any time by replying to our email.
