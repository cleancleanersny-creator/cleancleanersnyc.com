Airbnb Cleaning in NYC: your questions

How fast can you turn a unit around?

Most studios and one bedrooms are done within the usual window between check out and check in. Tell us your times when you book.

Do you wash the linens?

We make the beds with your clean linens and strip the used ones. Ask us about laundry options.

Do you restock supplies?

Yes, we restock the items you leave for guests, like toilet paper and soap.

Will you tell me about damage?

Yes, we let you know right away if we find anything broken, stained or missing.

Do I need to be there?

No. Most hosts give us a lock code or key box.
